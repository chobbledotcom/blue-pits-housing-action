{%- comment -%} Sets trustpilot_total and trustpilot_average (one decimal place) from the reviews collection, for the compliments page and the ratings band to word in their own sentences. Stored reviews are all from Trustpilot, the only source in config review_sources. With no reviews, trustpilot_total is 0 and callers leave the rating out. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign trustpilot_total = collections.reviews.size -%} {%- if trustpilot_total > 0 -%} {%- assign _rating_sum = 0 -%} {%- for _review in collections.reviews -%} {%- assign _rating_sum = _rating_sum | plus: _review.data.rating -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign trustpilot_average = _rating_sum | times: 1.0 | divided_by: trustpilot_total | round: 1 -%} {%- endif -%}
