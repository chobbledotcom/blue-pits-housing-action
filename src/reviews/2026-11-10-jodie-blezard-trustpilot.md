So grateful for the opportunity to be part of Bluepits!

I would like to share my personal experience from my social work placement at Bluepits. Starting my placement I was always made to feel welcome and well supported within the team. It was more than just somewhere to work it was something meaningful to me, it was enjoyable and will always have a special place in my heart as part of my journey in my career. I found the placement to be incredibly rewarding giving me the opportunity to support individuals in developing and maintaining independent skills and encouraging them to take part in sessions at the landings. I really enjoyed getting involved in the activity classes which included socialising, arts and crafts, games and party’s. I feel I built some lovely relationships whilst also learning and will always be grateful for the experience Bluepits offered me. I have since qualified and moved in to the local authority but feel if the opportunity ever arises in a job change then I would love to return to Bluepits as it’s such a special place that I really do miss.Thank you all at Bluepits for the wonderful experience.
