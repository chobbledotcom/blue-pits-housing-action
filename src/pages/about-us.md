      # About Us
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      Blue Pits Housing Action provides support to all adult groups, including those with mental health needs and those with a dual diagnosis of learning disability or substance misuse.

      We provide a highly-skilled, diverse and experienced workforce and involve users of the service and any appropriate family members or carers, consulting with them to agree individual, needs-led, holistic support plans and risk assessments.

      People are provided with opportunities to learn and develop skills through educational and awareness workshops, while our devoted staff facilitate their development and wellbeing.

      We promote multi-disciplinary assistance by working with primary, secondary and tertiary health and social care providers.

      We're proud of the progress made by every single tenant at Blue Pits Housing Action - care and compassion from our skilled and knowledgeable staff support people as they gain their independence and learn important life skills. We strive to surpass the care provided by other mental health and learning disability support agencies and this is reflected in the levels of satisfaction felt by our tenants.

      ## Who we work with

      We work closely with our associated businesses, [Newbarn Ltd](https://www.newbarnltd.co.uk) and [Freehold Cottage](https://www.freeholdcottage.com). Tenants of Blue Pits Housing Action and Newbarn Ltd share the Landings WellbeingHub, and staff from all three train and celebrate together.

      The Hygiene Bank gives us donated toiletries for the tenants we support. In 2025 we completed a five-year strategic plan with the Cranfield Trust.

      ## Our staff

      Our staff train together throughout the year. Recent training has included crisis prevention (CPI), cyber awareness, oral health and palliative care with Springhill Hospice, and support workers have completed qualifications including the Level 2 Diploma in Care and Level 4 Lead Practitioner in Adult Care.

      Each month staff vote for a team member of the month. In May 2025 we held our first annual staff celebration with Newbarn Ltd and Freehold Cottage, with awards voted for by tenants and colleagues.

      ## Fundraising

      Our staff and tenants raise money for things that make a difference to the people we support. In 2024 we finished raising enough for defibrillators at three of our buildings.

      Our "Everyone deserves cake" fundraiser, which ran through 2025, pays for every tenant we support to get a birthday card, cake and present.

      Tenants make wooden crafts such as benches, bird boxes and bug hotels, and sell them at craft fairs. The money goes back into our charitable pot to buy materials and run more activities.
